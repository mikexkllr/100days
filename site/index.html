100 Days & Beyond

Show up for 100 days.

A 100-day challenge you keep with friends: one check-in a day, a streak that grows into a flame, leagues, and a meal plan and shopping list for your whole household.

With friends

Find friends from your contacts or by username, nudge and cheer them, keep a streak together and climb the weekly leagues.

Eat to plan

Log meals from a photo, a barcode or a few words. Let the AI plan your week — for everyone in the household — and get one shared shopping list.

Open source

Every line is on GitHub. Run your own server if you like; the app connects to it.

Support

Questions, problems, ideas: write to info@my-diary.app, or open an issue on GitHub. We answer in English or German.

Premium and subscriptions

Premium gives you a much larger monthly AI allowance and the AI coach. You buy and cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account; see the terms for how renewals work.

Deleting your account

Settings → Account → Delete account, or by email.
