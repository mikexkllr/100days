2. What we process

2.1 Account

  • Sign-in with Google or Apple: your email address (with Apple possibly a private relay address), your name if the provider shares it, and a user ID generated by our authentication service.
  • Sign-up with email: your email address, your password (stored by our authentication service only as a salted hash, which we cannot read), and a user ID. We send one email from our own mail server to confirm the address.
  • Profile: the display name and emoji you choose, and optionally a username. Other signed-in users can see your profile so they can find and add you.

2.2 Your challenge and diary

Everything you log is stored as entries in your feed: check-ins, habits, workouts, your challenge, and — if you use them — body measurements (such as weight), nutrition targets and your food diary. Each entry is either

  • visible to your friends (check-ins, workouts, your challenge without body data, nudges and cheers), or
  • private (body measurements, targets, food diary, private habits): readable only by you.

Body measurements, nutrition and diet information can be health data (Art. 9 GDPR). We process them only because you enter them, to show them back to you, and on the basis of your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time by deleting the entries or your account.

2.3 Friends, leagues and finding people

  • Friends: friend requests, friendships and invite codes.
  • Public league: unless you switch it off in the settings, your name, emoji and weekly XP are shown to the up to 30 people in your weekly league group.
  • Address book matching (optional): only if you allow access to your contacts and start a search. The App sends the email addresses in your address book to our server (phone numbers stay on your phone). The server turns each into a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA256 with a secret key), compares the hashes with those of users who chose to be findable, returns the matches and discards what you sent. It does not store your address book. To keep this from being abused, the server counts how many searches you made per day.
  • Blocking and reporting: if you block someone, we store whom you blocked, so that neither of you can find or add the other; the other person is not told. If you report someone, we store your report — the reason, the text you add, the name and username the person showed at the time, and when — so that we can review it. Only we can read reports.
  • Being findable (optional): if you switch it on, a keyed hash of the email address your sign-in provider verified is stored so friends can find you. Switching it off deletes it.

2.4 Households, meal plans and shopping lists

If you create or join a household, its meal plan, recipes and shopping lists are stored and are visible to all members of that household. If you switch on sharing your targets with the household, your calorie and protein targets and the diet, dislikes and allergies you enter are visible to its members so they can plan portions for you. How you like to cook (ready-made, everyday or from scratch) is stored with them. Switching sharing off deletes these values from the household. Your diary is never part of a household.

2.5 AI features

When you use an AI feature — estimating a meal from a photo or description, planning a week or a recipe, talking to Funki, or (with Premium) the coach — the App sends only what that feature needs to our server, which forwards it to the Google Gemini API and returns the answer:

  • meal photos: before sending, the App scales the photo down and re-encodes it, which removes its metadata such as location and camera details. The photo is not stored, neither on our server nor in your diary; only the entries you confirm are saved;
  • planning: the days and meals to plan, the first names of the household members and — if they share them — their targets, diet, dislikes and allergies, the cooking level you picked, your wishes and recently planned dish titles; for a recipe, also the name of the dish;
  • Funki (the chat and "ask Funki"): what you type, and what Funki looks up to answer it, only when he needs it: today's habits and streak, your food diary of a day with your targets, the household's meal plan, recipes and shopping list, your training plan and last workouts, and your friends' display names with their streaks. When you ask him to, Funki enters or changes things for you — diary entries, check-ins, meals and their recipes, list items, what is always at home, your food preferences, the exercises and days of your training plan; each change is shown and can be undone;
  • coach (Premium): your streak, day number, habits and friends' first names.

Our server stores only the number of AI tokens you used per month, to apply the free allowance. Google processes the content to generate the answer and may keep it for a limited time for abuse monitoring under its terms.

Your own API key (optional): if you enter your own key for Google, Anthropic or OpenAI, it is stored only in your phone's secure storage, and AI requests go directly from your phone to that provider under the terms you agreed with them. We receive neither the key nor the requests.

2.6 Barcode lookup

When you scan a product barcode, the App sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts to look up the product. Open Food Facts receives your IP address with the request.

2.7 Push notifications (optional)

If you allow notifications, the App registers a device token with Firebase Cloud Messaging and stores it on our server, together with which kinds of notifications you want. We use it to tell you about friend requests, nudges, cheers and your friends' activity. To limit notifications, the server records when a notification was sent. Reminders you set for yourself are scheduled on your phone and do not use our server.

2.8 Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play. The App passes the store's proof of purchase to our server, which checks it with Apple or Google and stores the product, the store's transaction reference and the expiry date. We never see your payment details.

2.9 Crash reports

When the App crashes or runs into an error it did not expect, it sends a crash report to Sentry, a service for finding and fixing such errors. A report contains the error and where in the App it happened (stack trace), the App version, the device model, the operating system and its version, the language, app and device events shortly before the error (such as going to the background or losing the network), and a random installation ID that Sentry generates. It contains no name, email address, user ID or content you entered, and Sentry discards your IP address. We use the reports only to fix errors.

2.10 What we do not do

  • No advertising, no advertising IDs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • No location, microphone or browsing history.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, feed, friends and household data: until you delete it or your account.
  • AI token counts: per month, until you delete your account.
  • Purchase records: as long as the subscription runs and as required by German tax law (up to 10 years).
  • Blocks: until you lift them or delete your account. Reports: as long as we need them to handle the report and to prevent repeated abuse; if an account is deleted, the report keeps the name and username shown in it, but not the link to the account.
  • Crash reports: deleted by Sentry after at most 90 days.

Deleting your account (Settings → Account → Delete account) removes your account and everything linked to it from our database immediately. Details: Delete your account.
