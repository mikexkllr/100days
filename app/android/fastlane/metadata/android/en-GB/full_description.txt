Pick what you want to change. Show up every day for 100 days. Do it with friends.

100 Days & Beyond turns a big goal into one small check-in a day: no sugar, reading, training, cold showers — whatever your 100 days are about. Your streak grows into Funki, a flame with a face that changes form as you keep going, and the app makes the good days feel good.

DAILY CHECK-INS
• Your habits for today on one screen, checked off with a press and hold
• A streak, XP and levels, milestones on days 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 …
• Rest days and freezes, so one bad day does not end everything
• A home screen widget with your streak

WITH FRIENDS
• Find friends from your contacts, by username, by link or QR code
• See their check-ins, cheer them on, nudge them when they are late
• Friend streaks and weekly quests you do together
• Weekly leagues with divisions: climb from Bronze to the top

EAT TO PLAN
• Log a meal from a photo, a barcode or a few words — the AI estimates calories and protein, you confirm
• Let the AI plan your week, for everyone in your household, sized to each person's targets
• One shared shopping list, built from the plan, ticked off live in the shop
• Weekly or your own shopping rhythm

FREE AND PREMIUM
The app is free with a monthly AI allowance. Premium gives you a much larger allowance and the AI coach. You can also use your own API key from Google, Anthropic or OpenAI instead.

OPEN SOURCE
Every line of the app and the server is on GitHub. You can run your own server, and the app connects to it.

Privacy: no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Meal photos are not stored. Details: https://mikexkllr.github.io/100days/privacy.html

Calorie values and AI estimates are for orientation, not medical advice.
